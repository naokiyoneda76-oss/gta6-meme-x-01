# Design brief — "Booked in Leonida" (designer agent, 2026-11-21)

Final 1024x1024. Photorealistic mugshot; flat cartoon ghost (official mascot: chubby white, thick black outline, two black four-point sparkle eyes, NO mouth, small notch at bottom centre, short arms) front-on against a grey height-chart wall (lines only in the generated image; numbers added in post: 5'0" 5'6" 6'0", Liberation Sans Bold 34px, left edge). Ghost ~78% width, head top ~8% from top, head top lands at the 5'2" position between the 5'0" and 5'6" lines (that tick slightly longer). Expression: deadpan, quietly proud — chin up ~4 degrees (the excuse line reads as a boast). Arms down at sides, tips gripping the placard's left/right edges, never covering its centre.

Placard (drawn in post onto the generated BLANK placard): 860x350, white #FAFAF7, black border 10px, square corners. Font: Oswald Bold (Google Fonts, OFL); fallback Liberation Sans Bold. Field strip: Liberation Mono Bold. Layout top→bottom:
- LEONIDA PD — 68px, wide letter spacing, centred
- rule 4px
- GRAND THEFT AUTO — 118px (largest)
- — REAL LIFE — — 96px
- EXCUSE: "13 YEARS OF PRACTICE" — 74px, one line
- rule 4px
- ID NO. 0013 | DATE 11.19.2026 | CASE: GTA VI — 42px Mono, 3 cells split by vertical rules (CASE: GTA VI satisfies the check agent's in-image GTA VI requirement; date = launch day; ID 0013 = 13 years)

480px survival: meaningful text never below 40px at 1024 (field strip 42px ≈ 20px at 480 = OK; chart numbers are atmosphere only).
Grain goes on BEFORE the text layer (text stays crisp). Ghost white may be toned to #F5F5F2 max. Flash shadow of the ghost on the wall; soft placard shadow on the ghost's belly; light AO where arms meet the placard.
Acceptance: placard blank/white/flat/front-on (reject >3° tilt; minor trapezoid absorbed by 4-point perspective on the text layer); ghost checklist — sparkle eyes, no mouth, thick uniform outline, bottom notch; up to 2 regenerations. Fallback: extract the ghost from assets/mark-1024.png (mask out magenta circle and bubbles) and composite onto a generated wall+blank-placard scene.
